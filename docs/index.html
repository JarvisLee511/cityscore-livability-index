01 What the sliders are showing

Moving a slider is not a what-if. It is the whole finding.

Across all 10,000 weightings the median area moves 8 places out of 17. Only 2 of the 17 hold their position within three places no matter how the index is weighted.

Change nothing but the direction of the two arguable indicators — whether expensive housing is good or bad, whether density is good or bad — and ranks shift by up to 5 places on their own.

So a ranked table of seventeen numbers promises a precision this data does not have. What survives is narrower, and it is in band 03.

The instrument above is honest about its own limits: normalisation is min–max on n = 17, so the best and worst area are pinned to exactly 1.0 and 0.0 on every indicator by construction. Some of the spread is the scaling, not the county.

02 The crime input had to be rebuilt

A 16,000-row export from a service that returns at most 16,000 rows.

Crime carries the largest weight in the composite, so it got checked first. The original file had exactly 16,000 rows. The county's ArcGIS feature service declares maxRecordCount: 16000. And that service reports 23,914 incidents inside the same date window the export covers.

The export held about two thirds of its own window and stopped where the API stopped, not where the data did. Nothing suggests the missing third is spread evenly across ZIP codes, so a rate computed from it is not a rate.

The rebuild pages past the cap: all 25,829 incidents reported in calendar 2025, each assigned to a ZIP area by point-in-polygon against Census boundaries. Against the original column it lands at Spearman +0.115 — close to unrelated.

03 What survives

Three claims the data actually carries.

  • 99.9%of all possible weightings put Occoquan (22125) in the top five.
  • >90%of them put Manassas/Sudley (20109) and Triangle (22172) in the bottom five.
  • —Everything between those two ends is a restatement of the weights that produced it.

Two areas are also too small to rate: Occoquan has 917 residents and Catharpin 1,124, so their crime rates rest on a few dozen incidents and their Poisson intervals span most of the table.
